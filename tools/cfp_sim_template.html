How it works

Remaining games: every FBS game left on the schedule, from your model's latest grades. Title-game spots already clinched are fixed; the rest use conference record, then head-to-head, then the model's grade, which only approximates the real tiebreakers. Committee: starts from the latest CFP top 25 ({{RANKDATE}}); a rivalry-week loss drops a ranked team about five spots, a title-game loss about 2.5, a title-game win lifts it about two, with a little random noise. The field is the four power-conference champions, the highest-ranked Group of 6 champion and the next seven teams, seeded in ranking order, with the top four getting byes. Your model has not beaten closing lines in its backtest, so read these as the model's view, not betting odds.
