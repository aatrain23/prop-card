NFL Team Grades

Each team's nfelo rating plus its QB adjustment and an injury adjustment, turned into a model spread for every game and compared with the market line, plus a separate over/under model and home field numbers. Information only: across 2016–2026 the spread model and the over/under model have both missed by slightly more than the closing lines have.

A quick estimate: the rating gap (team rating + QB adjustment + injuries, times the model's 1.048 weight) plus the typical home edge (see the Home field tab). The weekly model also adds game context (byes, travel, divisional games) and rest, so its numbers on the "This week" tab can differ by a point or two.

Model spread against the market line when the model ran. Gap = how far apart they are; A and B grades are the model's bets. Tap a game for every live line in the Line Shop.

Model total against the market total when the model ran, with each team's projected points (the model total split by the model spread). The total comes from each team's scoring and defense ratings (updated after every game), both QBs, the roof, wind and cold, and injuries on offense and defense. It never looks at the betting line.

How the over/under model has done

Walk-forward results against closing totals, by how far the model was from the line. 52.4% breaks even at -110. No grade has cleared that, so treat these as information, not bets.

Home edge is the estimate for each team, in points. It starts from how much better the team has done at home than on the road over the last 10 seasons compared with what the model expected (Raw), then keeps only the share of team-to-team differences that isn't luck (about a third). This week is the home field number actually inside this week's model spread: the league edge adjusted for travel, time zones, byes, surface and divisional games.

Tested on 2018 through this season, adding team-by-team home edges did not make the spread model more accurate, so the grades and spreads don't use them.
