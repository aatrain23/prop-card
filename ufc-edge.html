Method, distance and rounds

Method, distance and round calls from the analysts, as long as none of them conflict. At least one analyst names the method and nobody names a different one: decision, KO, submission, or any mix of KO, submission and "inside the distance", which becomes the inside-the-distance prop. A decision call alongside a finish call cancels out. The matching goes / doesn't go the distance line is shown as the same read. Only decision props are bet: 0.5u, and they need a model edge of 0 or better. Finish props (KO, submission, inside the distance, rounds) are listed as leans, not bet: in the props backtest they lost money at every model edge, and Action Network's KO and inside-the-distance picks lost about 41%. Age is the strongest fighter-level signal. In near pick'em fights the younger fighter returned +7.8% (2010–21) and +10.7% (2022–26); the older fighter lost 16% and 19%. Fighters 35+ facing someone under 32 lost about 21% since 2022. Long layoffs and two-fight skids also hurt underdogs. Reach and stance did not hold up.

Method props are a tax. Every method market lost money; favorite by decision was the least bad at -2.8%. Stick to moneylines unless you have a specific read.
