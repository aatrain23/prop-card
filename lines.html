Line Shop

Type the bets you're thinking about and see every book's line and price side by side: FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and Fanatics (from Action Network's public odds), plus Novig and Kalshi (their public market data, every alt line). Game lines: spread, total, moneyline and team totals (UFC: fighter moneyline, total rounds and method). NFL also has player props (Novig and Kalshi). Nothing is ever placed.

Spread: team and number ("Duke -12.5"). Moneyline: team and "ML". Total: either team (or both) and o/u with the number ("Duke u41.5"). The number is the line you want: any book at that line or better counts, and the best price among those is highlighted. Prices worse than -150 are shown in red. Team total: team, TT and o/u ("Vikings TT o20.5"). First half or first quarter: add 1H or 1Q ("Duke 1H -3.5", "Bears 1Q +0.5", "Duke Clemson 1H o24.5", "Oregon 1H ML"); first halves at FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Fanatics, theScore, Novig and Kalshi; first quarters at FanDuel, BetMGM, Fanatics, theScore and Kalshi (Kalshi’s half and quarter winner markets lose on a tie). First half team totals too ("Duke 1H TT o17.5"; books and Kalshi). NFL player props: player, o/u and number, and the stat ("Jefferson o82.5 rec yds", "Purdy u1.5 pass TDs", "McCaffrey anytime TD", "Kittle 2+ TD"); stats: pass/rush/rec yds, rush+rec yds, pass+rush yds, receptions, completions, pass or rush attempts, pass TDs, INTs, longest reception, rush or completion, TDs, first TD scorer ("McCaffrey first TD"), kicker field goals made and kicking points ("Reichard o1.5 FGs", "Reichard o7.5 kicking pts"). Leave out the number to get the main line. UFC: a fighter's name (or name and "ML") for the moneyline, both fighters and o/u for total rounds ("Prochazka Stirling o1.5"). Methods: "Prochazka by KO", "Stirling sub", "Prochazka dec"; the fight as a whole: "Prochazka Stirling ends by KO", "Prochazka Stirling goes distance" or "doesn't go distance".
