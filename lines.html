Line Shop

Type the bets you're thinking about and see every book's line and price side by side: FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and Fanatics (from Action Network's public odds), plus Novig and Kalshi (their public market data, every alt line). Game lines only: spread, total, moneyline. Nothing is ever placed.

Spread: team and number ("Duke -12.5"). Moneyline: team and "ML". Total: either team (or both) and o/u with the number ("Duke u41.5"). The number is the line you want: any book at that line or better counts, and the best price among those is highlighted. Prices worse than -150 are shown in red.
