Live stats come from ESPN and refresh every 30 seconds. The tick mark on each bar is the line. The rule: one bet per game. An expert's Under in rushing yards, receiving yards, completions or tackles + assists at -150 or better, within a point of the expert's line; if a game has none, an expert's Over that the model rates 6+ points better. Player props are half a unit (to win $50); spreads, totals, team totals and moneylines are 1 unit (to win $100). "Playable to" is the worst price that still fits the rule. Percentages are the price as an implied win chance. "Why this bet" explains the pick: what the model expected and what the expert said, where their write-up was available. When you add a bet from the pre-game board you can write your own reason too; it shows first. For entertainment, not betting advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.
