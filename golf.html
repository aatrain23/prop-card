How to watch

Each golfer's name links to his ESPN page with his live scorecard. PGA TOUR LIVE on ESPN+ streams four featured groups and marquee holes every round, so a golfer outside those groups shows up only in the main broadcast or his scorecard.

Leaderboard

Entered FanDuel PGA lineups, scored hole by hole from ESPN's leaderboard every 60 seconds. FanDuel points use CourseFit's table: eagle or better 7, birdie 3.1, par 0.5, bogey −1, double or worse −3; +0.6 for each birdie-or-better after the first in a streak, +0.3 for a bounce-back after a bogey, +5 for a bogey-free round and +4 for 5+ birdies in a round. Position points (30 for 1st down to 1 for 31st–40th, ties get the full amount) count live off the current leaderboard, like FanDuel's, so they move until the event is final. On best-6-of-7 weeks the lowest current score is dropped from each lineup's total, as FanDuel does live; the projection drops the lowest-projected golfer. Projected final = points so far + the golfer's CourseFit projection for the holes he has left; before the cut, weekend holes count at his cut chance (once he has finished 36 holes, that chance comes from where he sits against the projected cut), and a golfer who misses the cut adds nothing. Streaks follow hole-number order, so a round started on the 10th tee can be a few tenths off until it's done. The cut line is worked out here as the top 65 and ties after 36 holes. Projected cut: the 36-hole score of the 65th golfer (top 65 and ties) if everyone finished where they stand now. It moves through rounds 1 and 2 and becomes the real cut line once the second round is in. For entertainment, not betting advice. Play only what you can afford to lose.
